    Specification — the direction mechanism

    1. A brief enters at sense, where the surface is reduced to twelve lines: what it is, the one job, the mode, who reads it and how often, what must not break, one physical sentence about the room it is used in, and the reflex — the page this category always ships, written down so the next station can keep it off the list.

    2. At direct, the model derives seven grounded candidate directions from the audience's own cultural material, ordered by its own sense of resonance. It does not then choose. A die — a SHA-256 of a printed key, no network, no dependencies, the same deal on any machine — assigns the index. The assignment never lands on an ungrounded entry; it only refuses the argmax.

    3. The same roll deals three challengers from a deck of born-designed graphic systems : one graphic, one interaction, one atmosphere. Each is fused with the product before it is judged — the world supplies form and system grammar, the product supplies every fact, clarity wins conflicts — and then weighed on exactly two axes: audience identification, product clarity. Wins both, it becomes the build. Holds one, it stays as a full alternate. Loses both, it is declined and still donates the one discipline the assigned direction lacked.

    4. The bound direction is written down twice at contract: as a five-block promise in the artifact's own source, and as a token file the build can only spend from. At review the render is handed across a boundary to a reviewer with no share in the build's optimism, which audits each promise block as kept, softened or broken and returns one of four dispositions : recapture, rebuild, fix, ship.

    Specification — the material

    5. Under every world sits a material contract: seed hue, colour strategy, type roles, ratio, density, corner, elevation, motion physics, imagery, component character, three rationed signature moves, voice, and the specific ways that language turns to slop. Seven of them ship with the skill. All seven compile to the same token names, so changing the language of a surface is one file, and linting it is one grep.

    6. Switch the control and the same figure re-inks. Ground, ink, rule, accent, corner and density below are the real token values, read out of the file named under the specimen. The face is named rather than loaded — this page ships two faces, not nine.

    Specification — the audit

    7. A direction described as radical ships conservative when nobody holds it to its own words. So the promise is written into the artifact before the first line of markup, in five blocks under 150 words, and it survives the production build. The review does not ask whether the page is good. It asks, block by block, whether the render kept it.

    8. The reviewer runs in a fresh context. It gets the request, the promise, captures at 390 / 768 / 1440, the mechanical bans report and the world card — not the transcript that produced the build. Order is fixed: evidence, promise audit, behavioural floor, then a P0/P1/P2 rubric graded on the worst sustained band, not the average.

    9. The last question is the one that decides whether anything was designed: for any similar brief, would I have produced this? Name what changed. If nothing changed, the work is competent and forgettable, and that is the verdict this skill exists to hand down.

    Specification — the seats

    10. This sheet carries four sponsor seats, and four of them are open. They sit in the band at the top of the page, which is the plate inverted: the one element here that is not drawn in hairline on paper, because an advertisement that pretends to be part of the document is a worse advertisement and a worse document.

    11. All four seats are on screen the whole time. The band rotates a spotlight, never the contents — nothing anyone paid for is ever hidden behind a timer, and a reader never waits a cycle to see who is here. The motion stops when you point at it, when you tab into it, when it scrolls out of view, when the tab goes to the background, and entirely if your system asks for reduced motion.

    12. Until a seat sells, the band shows what buying one looks like: the drafting cursor crosses to an open seat, presses, and the dashed frame closes into a solid line as a mark inks in. The filled seat is tagged specimen for as long as it is one. No borrowed logos stand in for sponsors we do not have — a name in that band is a public claim about somebody else's company, and this whole page exists to argue that invented proof is a defect.
