# Mechanism as a Live Model

## Form
A first viewport staged around a small simulated world that behaves the way the product's mechanism behaves -- a membrane that sags and tears under load for a load-testing tool, a flock that re-forms around a removed node for an orchestrator, a counter that settles for billing -- built model-first (simulation.md), running unattended, answering the visitor's hand, with the one-sentence offer and the action set into its frame.

## Spark
You drag a corner of the thing and it stretches, holds, then gives; you let go and it settles; and the sentence beside it says the product does exactly that to your traffic, and you believe it before reading the rest.

## Grammar
- Staging/hierarchy: the model owns the first viewport at the scale the mechanism has in life; the offer sentence and the one action sit beside or inside its frame at equal weight; nothing generic sits above them; the model card is the region's header comment and says what it proves.
- Sequence/attention: the model is already running when the page lands (idle behavior); the visitor touches it, feels the rule, reads the sentence that names the rule, takes the action; proof sections below show the real product doing the same thing on real data.
- Controls/state: pointer, keyboard-reachable control, or scroll is the input; parameters sit in one visible block when the surface is read mode (a slider for load, a count) and are hidden on persuade; the model is labelled as a model, never as the product or as live data; its settled state is the reduced-motion frame.
- Adaptation: at 390 the count and resolution drop before the sentence does; the model never pushes the action below the fold; no JS shows the settled still frame; keyboard reaches the action first, then the model's one control.

## Web leverage
A canvas or SVG loop that integrates and constrains a handful of objects from a seeded state, skinned from the page tokens and re-lit on theme change, with the same composition painted as a still under it for first paint and reduced motion.

## Fits
Landing and explainer surfaces for products whose value cannot be felt by embedding the product itself (infrastructure, load, scheduling, consensus, queues, anything that needs an account or real traffic) -- the case first-viewport-is-the-product-running names as its trap; read-mode concept pages where a rule is better computed than drawn. The trap: a model that proves nothing (canvas wallpaper), a pasted library demo in its own palette, or a metaphor so literal it becomes the mascot; a model on an operate surface is theater.
