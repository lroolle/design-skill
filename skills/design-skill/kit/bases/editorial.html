Field systems

The night shift found the delay the summary had averaged away.

A sample account of why a four-number status row could not replace the events underneath it.

By the fixture desk6 min read

The daily report said the route was on time. The event record showed a different system: one handoff absorbed ninety-four minutes, then three later stages recovered enough time to flatten the average.

The useful question was not whether the route was usually fast. It was where a person could no longer tell whether the next handoff had happened. That point belonged in the interface as a row, an owner, and missing evidence.

What changed

The next report keeps the population visible, sorts by worst variance, and gives each unresolved event a plain recovery action. Aggregate facts remain above the table, but never instead of it.
