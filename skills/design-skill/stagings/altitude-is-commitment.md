# Altitude Is Commitment

## Form
A whole product staged as ground seen from above, where far out the work reads as silent shapes, mid-height surfaces status and counts, and full tooling appears only at close range; the address of any view is coordinates and height.

## Spark
From high up the project is quiet geography; you dive toward the one district that looks wrong, watch counts resolve into handles as you descend, land where the fix is a drag away, then climb until it is all silent shapes again.

## Grammar
- Staging/hierarchy: the ground plane owns everything; at each altitude exactly one register of information leads, and controls exist only at the altitude where they act.
- Sequence/attention: descent narrows -- shapes, then states, then handles; attention is protected from tooling until the dive declares intent.
- Controls/state: continuous zoom with named landing altitudes; every view is a shareable coordinate-and-height URL; edits exist only below the tooling threshold; a changed region stays marked when you climb.
- Adaptation: phones keep the same altitudes with stepped zoom; keyboard users dive and climb by named level; screen readers get the altitude's register as a list.

## Web leverage
A zoomable scene graph swapping information registers by scale threshold, with viewport state carried in the URL and semantic lists mirrored per altitude.

## Fits
App shells for spatial or graph-shaped products (infrastructure maps, boards, canvases, site maps), portfolios staged as territory. The trap: a product with no natural geography, where the map is a metaphor painted over a list and every altitude shows the same thing at a different size.
