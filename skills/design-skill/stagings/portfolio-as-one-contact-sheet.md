# Portfolio as One Contact Sheet

## Form
A body of work staged as a single continuous contact sheet: every piece at thumbnail scale on one plane in chronological or series order, no categories, no hero, and a piece opens by enlarging in place while the sheet stays visible around it.

## Spark
You scroll one long sheet of small frames, a run of three catches you, you tap one and it grows to working size right there with the rest dimmed but present, and you can see what came before and after without leaving.

## Grammar
- Staging/hierarchy: the sheet owns the whole product; frames are equal at rest, order is the only hierarchy; the enlarged piece owns the center while the sheet stays as context; the name and contact sit in a thin fixed margin.
- Sequence/attention: scan the sheet, stop on a run, enlarge one, step to its neighbors, collapse back; attention never leaves the body of work for an about page.
- Controls/state: a frame at rest, a frame enlarged in place with title and one line, neighbors stepped by arrow keys or swipe; a series is a labelled run, not a folder; loading is grey frames at the true aspect ratios; the sheet never paginates.
- Adaptation: phones keep the sheet at two or three columns and the enlarged piece full width with the sheet scrolled behind; keyboard steps frames; reduced motion enlarges without zooming.

## Web leverage
A virtualized masonry or grid of true-aspect frames with an in-place enlarge via shared-element or view transition, and per-piece URLs so the sheet is deep-linkable.

## Fits
Portfolio and showcase for photographers, illustrators, studios, product shots. The trap: work that needs its own case-study narrative (process, problem, outcome), where the sheet shows pictures and hides the thinking; and small bodies of work where the sheet is a dozen frames.
