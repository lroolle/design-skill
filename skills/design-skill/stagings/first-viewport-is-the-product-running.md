# First Viewport Is the Product Running

## Form
A landing page staged so the first viewport is the product itself, live on honest data, with the one-sentence offer and the action set into the product's own frame rather than above a screenshot of it.

## Spark
You land and the thing is already working: the search runs, the numbers move, you type into it before you have read the headline, and the headline beside it only tells you what you just felt.

## Grammar
- Staging/hierarchy: the running product owns the first viewport at working size; the offer sentence and the one action are set into its chrome or beside it at equal weight; nothing sits above them.
- Sequence/attention: touch the product, read the sentence, take the action; proof sections below show the product doing harder things, each on real data.
- Controls/state: the embedded product is real or a faithful sandbox, labelled as a sandbox where it is one; its states (loading, empty, error) are the product's own; the action is one verb visible without scroll at 390px.
- Adaptation: on phones the product shrinks to its mobile form, still interactive, with the sentence and action directly beneath; keyboard reaches the action first; reduced motion stills any live demo to a settled state.

## Web leverage
An embedded instance or sandboxed build of the product sharing the page, with a labelled synthetic dataset and its own state handling, not a video or a screenshot.

## Fits
Landing for tools, editors, search, data products -- anything whose value is felt in ten seconds of use. The trap: products that cannot be felt without an account or real data (the demo lies), and heavy demos that push the offer and action below the fold.
