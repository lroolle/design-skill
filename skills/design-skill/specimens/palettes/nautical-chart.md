# nautical chart -- buff paper, black, three depth blues, magenta

Provenance: a British Admiralty chart, 1970s print, buff paper; the
depth tints, the black soundings, the magenta of lights and
prohibited areas. Set by eye against a folded chart in daylight and a
scan.

## The irregularity

The blues are not a ramp of one blue: the shallowest tint is
green-leaning (215), the next is neutral blue (235), the deep-water
white is the paper itself. Land is buff-yellow (90), a different
material from the sea tints, not their complement. Magenta (335) is
the only warm chroma and it is reserved for things that matter at
night. Black is a printing black, warm (60).

## Inks

```
paper (deep water)  oklch(0.965 0.012 90)     --bg
shoal tint          oklch(0.930 0.030 215)    --surface       (green-blue, the 10 m line)
shallow tint        oklch(0.890 0.045 235)    --surface-2     (the 5 m line)
land                oklch(0.900 0.040 90)     extra: --land
black               oklch(0.230 0.010 60)     --fg
soundings           oklch(0.420 0.010 60)     --fg-2
faint               oklch(0.550 0.008 60)     --fg-3
grid                oklch(0.820 0.020 235)    --line          (blue, not gray: the lattice is sea)
contour             oklch(0.560 0.040 235)    --line-strong
magenta             oklch(0.560 0.220 335)    --accent
magenta soft        oklch(0.930 0.040 335)    --accent-soft
```

Semantic: danger is magenta (it already is on a chart), ok is the
paper, warn is the shallow tint. Info is the contour blue.

## Must not become

Navy and gold, or teal and white. The paper is buff, the blues are
tints of sea over it, and the accent is magenta -- three things no
finance or health reflex produces.
