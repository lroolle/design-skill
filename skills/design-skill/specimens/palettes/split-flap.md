# split flap -- black flaps, cream letters, one amber

Provenance: a Solari di Udine departure board, 1970s, in a railway
hall; the flaps are black-painted steel, the letters silkscreened in
cream, the line-number flaps in a yellow-amber, the hall light
falling across them. Set by eye against photographs; dark-first.

## The irregularity

The ground is painted steel: warm black (60), slightly glossy, so
`--surface` is the flap *catching light*, a lighter warm gray, not a
step up a ladder. The letters are cream (85), not white, and they are
the same cream at every size. The one chroma is the amber flap (80),
opaque, a physical part, so its soft form is the amber at lower
lightness (dirt), never a tint. The split line between flaps is a
darker black, the only "line".

## Inks

```
steel        oklch(0.180 0.006 60)      --bg
flap lit     oklch(0.230 0.006 60)      --surface
flap edge    oklch(0.270 0.007 60)      --surface-2
cream        oklch(0.900 0.030 85)      --fg
cream worn   oklch(0.700 0.025 85)      --fg-2
cream faint  oklch(0.580 0.020 85)      --fg-3
split        oklch(0.120 0.006 60)      --line          (darker than the ground)
frame        oklch(0.340 0.008 60)      --line-strong
amber        oklch(0.820 0.150 80)      --accent
amber dirt   oklch(0.300 0.050 80)      --accent-soft
```

Light theme: the printed timetable on the wall beside it
(timetable-paper).

## Must not become

Near-black + acid accent: the ground is warm and lit, the letters are
cream, the amber is a painted part at L 0.82, and nothing glows. The
motion is stepped (mechanical), never smooth.
