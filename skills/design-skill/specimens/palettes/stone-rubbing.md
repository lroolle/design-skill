# stone rubbing -- ink ground, paper mark, cinnabar seal

Provenance: an ink rubbing (拓片) from a Han-dynasty stele, mounted; the
collector's seal in cinnabar at the lower corner. Set by eye against a
museum reproduction under gallery light. The house kit's dark theme.

## The irregularity

The ground is the ink -- cool, slightly blue (250), matte, never pure
and never neutral. The characters are the paper left bare, so the
"text color" is a warm paper white (88), a different hue from the
ground. Where the tamping was uneven the ink thins to a gray that
still carries the blue; that is `--line`. The seal is the only chroma
and it sits *on* the paper, so on this ground it is a lighter, drier
red than the stamp on white paper.

## Inks

```
ink ground   oklch(0.170 0.008 250)     --bg
ink pooled   oklch(0.210 0.008 250)     --surface
ink thin     oklch(0.250 0.008 250)     --surface-2
paper        oklch(0.920 0.010 88)      --fg
paper worn   oklch(0.720 0.010 88)      --fg-2
paper faint  oklch(0.600 0.008 85)      --fg-3
tamping      oklch(0.300 0.006 250)     --line
tamping edge oklch(0.420 0.008 250)     --line-strong
seal         oklch(0.680 0.160 32)      --accent
seal ghost   oklch(0.280 0.050 32)      --accent-soft
```

## Must not become

Near-black + acid accent: the ground is a visible blue-black (L 0.17,
C 0.008), not 0.05; the red is matte and warm, not neon; no glow, no
grid, no mono labels. Text is paper, so it is warm; a cool white text
on this ground is the developer look.
