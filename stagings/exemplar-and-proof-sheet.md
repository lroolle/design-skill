# Exemplar and Proof Sheet

## Form
A bulk-edit screen staged as one item worked at full size beside a sheet of every other affected item rendered small and live, where the proofs that would break lift themselves out of the sheet before you commit.

## Spark
You tighten the title on the exemplar and three of two hundred proofs rise out of the grid -- the ones that would truncate -- you open one, add a fallback, and watch all three settle back.

## Grammar
- Staging/hierarchy: the exemplar owns the working half at full scale; the proof sheet fills the rest as a countable population with raised outliers ranked first.
- Sequence/attention: compose on the exemplar, sweep the sheet after each change; raised proofs interrupt the sweep exactly where the trouble is.
- Controls/state: every edit applies to the population live and provisionally; any proof can become the temporary exemplar or be excluded; commit states the final count and what was excluded.
- Adaptation: small screens alternate exemplar and sheet as two full views with a persistent outlier count; keyboard moves between raised proofs first.

## Web leverage
Virtualized live rendering of the affected set applying provisional state to hundreds of small previews on each input, with outlier detection by measured overflow.

## Fits
Dashboard and app-shell bulk operations, settings that cascade, template editors. The trap: using it for a single-item edit, where the sheet is empty theatre, or hiding the proofs behind a "preview" button so the outliers never raise themselves.
