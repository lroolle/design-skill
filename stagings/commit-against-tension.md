# Commit Against Tension

## Form
A destructive or irreversible action staged as a loaded mechanism: committing means dragging against visible resistance -- a band, a lever arc, a spring -- with the release point marked, where half pulls snap back harmless and full pulls fire.

## Spark
You drag the delete lever through its arc and feel the surface resist, the row you are about to remove dimming in preview, and one pixel past the mark the mechanism gives way and the row is gone.

## Grammar
- Staging/hierarchy: the mechanism sits beside the thing it will act on; the marked release threshold outranks decoration and stays visible mid-pull.
- Sequence/attention: attention rides the pull -- resistance rises, the marker approaches, the consequence previews before release; no dialog interrupts.
- Controls/state: past the threshold fires, short of it snaps back with nothing done; states are rest, pulling, past-threshold, fired, cooldown; undo is offered after firing where reversibility exists.
- Adaptation: touch and pointer both drive the pull; keyboard gets hold-to-charge with the same marker; reduced motion keeps the threshold and drops the spring.

## Web leverage
Pointer capture with spring interpolation mapping drag distance to rising resistance and a hard release threshold, with the consequence previewed live on the target.

## Fits
Forms and app-shell actions where a confirm dialog would otherwise appear: delete, send, publish, pay. The trap: using it on routine actions (it becomes a chore) or where undo is available (undo over confirmation wins; the mechanism is for the genuinely irreversible).
