# Can a template donate a mechanism without donating its reflex?

## Sources

Observed in one isolated ego-browser task space on 2026-09-01:

- [Framer Marketplace](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/)
- [Webflow Templates](https://webflow.com/templates)
- [Readymag Examples](https://readymag.com/examples) and
  [Templates](https://readymag.com/templates)
- [Squarespace Templates](https://www.squarespace.com/templates)
- [One Page Love](https://onepagelove.com/templates)
- [Minimal Gallery](https://minimal.gallery/templates/)

Land-book returned a bot-verification screen and contributed no
observation. The live pages below remain open in the browser task space
for the team; the claims here come from the rendered desktop first
viewport and semantic page tree. Mobile behavior is unverified.

## Shortlist

### 1. Blue Portfolio -- Readymag

Live: [Blue Portfolio](https://readymag.com/designs/5743252/)

- Mechanism: the subject word occupies the viewport as the primary
  object; the next region turns each letter of the claim into evidence.
- Task effect: identity is immediate and the page has one question to
  answer, but a visitor cannot yet see whose portfolio this is.
- Keep: make the subject itself carry hierarchy, then make the following
  region substantiate it instead of changing topics.
- Change: introduce owner, work, and next action before the word becomes
  a poster with no route.
- Do not copy: blue, the exact grotesk, the giant-word crop, or the
  Readymag copy-template control.
- Proposed landing: hold for a `read` or `experience` scenario; possible
  composition card, "claim becomes evidence".
- Team check: after ten seconds, is the missing owner productive
  curiosity or simply missing information?

### 2. Nordic / Atelier -- Readymag

Live: [Nordic](https://readymag.com/designs/5991161/)

- Mechanism: real source images overlap behind letter-shaped panels so
  the identity is assembled from the thing being sold, while menu and
  booking remain plain anchors.
- Task effect: the restaurant is felt before it is described; the
  booking path survives the expressive masthead.
- Keep: build one identity object from real product material and keep
  the primary action outside that object.
- Change: make the collage recompose into a deliberate mobile sequence,
  and replace template photography with attributable venue material.
- Do not copy: electric blue, pillow letters, bracketed mono labels, or
  the exact fan of photographs.
- Proposed landing: hold for a restaurant or venue scenario; possible
  composition card, "identity assembled from proof".
- Team check: at 390px, does the booking path remain visible while the
  assembled word still reads?

### 3. Orchid -- Framer

Live: [Orchid](https://orchid-template.framer.website/)

- Mechanism: a horizontal rail of projects is the first proof and the
  primary navigation surface; the offer and start-project action frame
  it instead of preceding it.
- Task effect: a visitor can inspect work immediately, but generic copy
  and synthetic project imagery make the proof untrustworthy as shipped.
- Keep: let discrete work objects occupy the first viewport at useful
  size, with one project action and one business action.
- Change: real projects, honest metadata, keyboard order, and a mobile
  rail that exposes more than one item without a gesture tutorial.
- Do not copy: cosmetic-product imagery, plus marks, oversized empty
  header space, or the agency sentence.
- Proposed landing: hold for an `experience` portfolio scenario; likely
  a raise to the existing `portfolio-as-one-contact-sheet` card rather
  than a new card.
- Team check: is the rail better than the existing contact-sheet
  composition on real work, or only more cinematic?

### 4. Floffice -- Framer

Live: [Floffice](https://floffice.framer.website/)

- Mechanism: one real place image surrounds a quiet offer slab; the tour
  action stays in the persistent navigation.
- Task effect: the service and physical reality are clear in one screen;
  location and availability are still one step away.
- Keep: a plain statement, one real environmental proof, one visit
  action. This is a useful standing exit, not a new world.
- Change: surface location and availability beside the tour action and
  reduce the blank slab once real copy is known.
- Do not copy: greenhouse-office photography, the five-item nav, muted
  capsule CTA, or the white-over-photo frame.
- Proposed landing: baseline for a `persuade` venue scenario; no deck
  change proposed.
- Team check: does the restraint still hold with real inventory and
  location facts in the first viewport?

### 5. Greven -- Webflow

Live: [Greven](https://greven-realestate-template.webflow.io/)

- Mechanism: the main property stage and a narrow independent proof rail
  share the first viewport; the rail can change evidence without moving
  the primary scene.
- Task effect: property and consultation are visible, but "find your
  perfect house" offers no actual search, price, or listing fact.
- Keep: the independent proof rail, if it carries verified alternates or
  state rather than thumbnails as decoration.
- Change: make search or a real listing the primary interaction and move
  verified price, place, and availability into the rail.
- Do not copy: luxury-night render, all-caps slogan, split word across
  the fold, or scroll prompt.
- Proposed landing: failure probe for a real-estate scenario; possible
  raise to `first-viewport-is-the-product-running`, not a new card.
- Team check: can the rail carry enough fact to earn its width on both
  desktop and mobile?

## Declined

- Creatie: a desktop-OS costume with badge and chip sprawl; the portfolio
  task is subordinated to a recognizable skin.
- Mondragon: the image ribbon is a generator without a product claim;
  project and social proof are squeezed into dashboard-like cards.
- Lucid: the scene is coherent, but the fictional dream-decoding product
  makes visual confidence impossible to separate from invented proof.
- Sorae and Archiste: strong photography carries category-standard
  agency staging; no mechanism was found beyond full-bleed atmosphere
  and a large name.

## Decision

Status remains `team-check`. Blue, Nordic, and Orchid are the strongest
mechanism donors; Floffice is the baseline; Greven is the failure probe.
Promote at most one mechanism into a frozen scenario after the team has
checked the live desktop and mobile behavior. No deck, recipe, kit, or
skill rule changes are earned by this browse alone.
